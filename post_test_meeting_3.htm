TEXT 1: RECOUNT TEXT Soal 1 - 6

My Experience Organizing the School Book Fair

Last semester, I had the chance to lead our school's annual charity book fair. As a 12th-grade student, I felt quite nervous because I had never managed a large event before. Our main objective was to collect donated books from students and raise money to support a rural elementary school library.

During the first two weeks, my team and I collected over 500 books from students and teachers. We sorted them into categories such as fiction, science, and children's books. To attract visitors, we created colorful posters around the school and promoted the event on our school’s official social media page.

On the event day, many students, parents, and teachers came to support us. We sold most of the books at very affordable prices. Although we were completely exhausted by the end of the day, seeing the empty tables and counting the funds we raised made all our hard work worthwhile.

Looking back, this experience taught me how to communicate better and work effectively in a team. It was not just about selling books, but about making a real difference together.

TEXT 2: NARRATIVE TEXT Soal 7 - 11

The Honest Fisherman and the Golden Shell

Long ago, in a quiet coastal village, lived a poor fisherman named Budi. Unlike other fishermen who used giant nets to catch as many fish as possible, Budi only caught what his family needed for the day. He believed that taking too much from the sea was greedy and harmful to nature.

One evening, Budi pulled up his net and found a heavy, shimmering golden shell. To his surprise, a gentle voice spoke from inside, "Kind fisherman, please release me back into the ocean, and I will protect your waters." Seeing that the shell belonged to the sea, Budi gently tossed it back without asking for any reward.

A greedy villager named Darno secretly watched from behind a rock. The next morning, Darno threw a massive net into the deep water and trapped the golden shell. He shouted, "Give me three chests of gold right now, or I will crush you!" The shell warned him that greed would bring ruin, but Darno demanded his wish immediately.

Suddenly, a giant wave rose from the calm sea, destroying Darno’s boat and washing away his heavy net. Darno barely survived and returned to the shore with nothing. Meanwhile, Budi’s modest nets were always filled with enough fish to feed his family, as the ocean blessed his honesty and respect.
