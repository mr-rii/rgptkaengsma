She was ambitious and wanted nothing more than to win the royal scholarship. On the night before the deadline, Maya noticed that her elderly neighbor, Mr. Harrison, was struggling in his workshop. His eyesight was failing, and he was weeping because he could not finish a simple cotton blanket that he needed to sell to pay his monthly rent.

Knowing that stepping away from her loom meant she might not finish her own elaborate tapestry in time, Maya hesitated for only a second. She quietly walked into Mr. Harrison's shop, took over his loom, and worked tirelessly through the night to complete his blanket. By dawn, Mr. Harrison’s rent was secured, but Maya’s tapestry remained unfinished. When the royal judge arrived, he learned of Maya's action and awarded her the scholarship anyway, stating that true mastery lies not just in technical skill, but in the heart of the artisan.
